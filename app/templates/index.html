Post-quantum signed certificates

QuantumCert issues degree certificates as PDFs signed with SLH-DSA (NIST FIPS 205), a hash-based signature scheme that stays secure even against attackers with large quantum computers. Anyone can check a certificate here, without an account.

Why trust this?

Trust rests on the issuer's public key, not on this website: each PDF carries its signed data (quantumcert.json) and can be verified offline with the published key (/api/keys). A forger without the issuer's private key cannot produce a valid signature, and any public key shipped inside a document is ignored.
